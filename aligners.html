Clear Aligner Care Tips - How to Maintain Your Dental Aligners

Proper aligner care is essential for successful clear aligner treatment and ensuring your aligners work effectively. Follow these important aligner care guidelines from our Delhi orthodontist:

  • Daily Aligner Cleaning: Clean your aligners every day with mild soap and lukewarm water (never hot water as it can warp aligners and damage the material).
  • Aligner Removal Before Eating: Always remove your aligners while eating or drinking anything other than water to protect your aligner trays from staining and damage.
  • Aligner Storage in Case: Keep your aligners in their protective case when not wearing them to prevent aligner damage, loss, or warping.
  • Aligner Wear Time Compliance: Wear your aligners 20–22 hours daily for best clear aligner treatment results and faster teeth movement.
  • Regular Teeth Cleaning: Brush and floss your teeth before reinserting aligners to maintain oral health during aligner treatment.
  • Avoid Staining: Don't drink colored beverages while wearing aligners to keep them clear and invisible.

Clear Aligner FAQ - Common Questions About Aligner Treatment in Delhi

❓ Is clear aligner treatment painful?
Clear aligner treatment is generally more comfortable than traditional braces. Most patients experience mild pressure and slight discomfort for a day or two after switching to a new aligner set, but this is not painful. Pain is not typical with aligner treatment.

❓ Can I get aligner treatment in Delhi if I have existing crowns or dental work?

❓ Is aligner treatment covered by dental insurance in India?
Some insurance plans cover part of clear aligner treatment cost as it's considered orthodontic treatment. We'll help you understand your aligner coverage and maximize your insurance benefits.

❓ How long do I need to wear clear aligners daily?
For optimal aligner treatment results, wear your aligners 20-22 hours daily. Remove them only for eating, drinking (except water), brushing, and flossing. Consistent aligner wear ensures faster teeth movement and better results.

❓ Can I eat with clear aligners?
No, always remove your clear aligners before eating or drinking anything except water. This protects the aligners from staining and damage. After eating, brush your teeth and clean your aligners before reinserting them.

❓ How often do I change my clear aligners?
Typically, you'll change to the next set of aligners every 1-2 weeks, depending on your treatment plan. Our orthodontist will provide a schedule and custom aligner trays for your teeth straightening journey.
