Dental Bridge

Advanced Dental Bridge Treatment in Delhi for Natural-Looking Smiles

A dental bridge is a fixed (non-removable) dental restoration used to replace one or more missing teeth. It literally “bridges” the gap between two teeth by attaching an artificial tooth (called a pontic) to the natural teeth or implants on either side.

Parts of a Dental Bridge

  • Abutment Teeth: The natural teeth (or implants) on both sides of the gap; they act as supports or anchors.
  • Pontic: The artificial tooth (or teeth) that fill the empty space.
  • Cement or Attachments: The material that permanently bonds the bridge to the abutment teeth.

Procedure (Step-by-Step)

  1. Examination and X-rays: The dentist checks the health of nearby teeth and gums.
  2. Tooth Preparation: The abutment teeth are trimmed slightly to make space for crowns.
  3. Impression Taking: A mold or 3D scan of your teeth is made for accurate fitting.
  4. Temporary Bridge: A temporary bridge is placed to protect the exposed teeth and gums.
  5. Permanent Bridge Placement: The final custom-made bridge is cemented permanently.

Time Required

Usually takes 2 to 3 dental visits over 1–2 weeks to complete.

Cost (Approximate)

India: ₹8,000 – ₹25,000 per tooth

USA: $500 – $2,500 per tooth

(Cost depends on material type and dentist’s experience.)

Types of Dental Bridges

  • Traditional Bridge (Most Common): Made of crowns on both sides of the missing tooth with a pontic in between. Best when you have strong natural teeth on both sides.
  • Cantilever Bridge: Attached to only one abutment tooth. Used when there’s only one supporting tooth next to the gap.
  • Maryland Bridge (Resin-Bonded): Uses metal or porcelain wings bonded to the back of nearby teeth. No tooth trimming needed; often used for front teeth.
  • Implant-Supported Bridge: Supported by dental implants instead of natural teeth. Best for multiple missing teeth.

Advantages of a Dental Bridge

  • Restores your smile and confidence
  • Improves chewing and speaking
  • Prevents remaining teeth from shifting
  • Faster and cheaper than implants
  • Feels natural once adjusted

Disadvantages / Risks

  • Requires grinding down healthy abutment teeth
  • Can increase sensitivity
  • May not last as long as implants
  • Harder to clean under the bridge
  • Can loosen or break if oral hygiene is poor

Care & Maintenance Tips

  • Brush twice daily using a soft toothbrush
  • Floss under the bridge using a floss threader
  • Visit your dentist every 6 months
  • Avoid biting hard foods like nuts or ice
  • Maintain good gum health to support the bridge

Lifespan

With good care, a dental bridge can last 5 to 15 years — sometimes longer.

Dental Bridge vs Implant: Cost, Types, Problems & Which Is Better in India

Missing teeth can affect your smile, chewing ability, and confidence. Two common solutions are dental bridges and dental implants. This guide compares both options, including costs in India, types, durability, pain, and how they compare with dentures.

What Is a Dental Bridge?

A dental bridge is a fixed restoration used to replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring an artificial tooth (pontic) to adjacent natural teeth or crowns.

Is a Dental Bridge Permanent?

  • Dental bridges are long-lasting but not always permanent.
  • Average lifespan: 8–15 years.
  • With good oral hygiene, some bridges last longer.

Types of Dental Bridges and Cost in India

  • Traditional Dental Bridge: Most common; uses crowns on adjacent teeth. Cost: ₹8,000 – ₹25,000 per tooth.
  • Cantilever Bridge: Supported by only one adjacent tooth. Cost: ₹10,000 – ₹20,000.
  • Maryland (Resin-Bonded) Bridge: Metal or ceramic wings bonded behind teeth; common for front teeth. Cost: ₹6,000 – ₹15,000.
  • Removable Dental Bridge: Can be removed for cleaning; less stable. Cost: ₹5,000 – ₹12,000.

Single Tooth Bridge Cost in India

  • 1-tooth bridge: ₹8,000 – ₹20,000
  • 2-tooth bridge: ₹16,000 – ₹40,000
  • 3-tooth bridge: ₹25,000 – ₹60,000

Crown / Bridge Teeth Price

  • Metal crown: ₹2,000 – ₹4,000
  • PFM crown: ₹3,000 – ₹6,000
  • Zirconia crown: ₹8,000 – ₹15,000

Dental Bridge Problems (Disadvantages)

  • Requires trimming healthy adjacent teeth.
  • Risk of decay under crowns if hygiene is poor.
  • Possible gum problems without proper care.
  • Does not preserve jawbone the way implants do.

Dental Bridge for Front Teeth (Anterior)

For anterior teeth, appearance is key. Ceramic or zirconia materials are preferred; Maryland bridges are commonly used for minimally invasive replacement.

Pros & Cons

Dental Bridge — Pros

  • Lower cost
  • Faster treatment
  • No surgery required

Dental Bridge — Cons

  • Damages healthy adjacent teeth
  • Limited lifespan

Dental Implant — Pros

  • Permanent solution
  • Preserves jawbone
  • Looks and feels natural

Dental Implant — Cons

  • Higher cost
  • Requires surgery
  • Longer treatment time

Pain: Bridge vs Implant

Bridges cause minimal discomfort. Implants involve surgery and mild post-op pain, usually manageable with medication. Implant pain is temporary; bridges are generally almost painless.

Which Is Best?

  • Single missing tooth: implant or single-tooth bridge.
  • Multiple missing teeth: bridge or implant-supported bridge.
  • Low budget: dentures.
  • Long-term solution and bone preservation: dental implant.

Dental Bridge Cost Without Insurance (India)

Most dental treatments in India are out-of-pocket. Typical ranges:

  • Single-tooth bridge: ₹8,000 – ₹20,000
  • 3-tooth bridge: ₹25,000 – ₹60,000

Public Opinion Summary

Common patient viewpoints: implants offer better long-term value; bridges are good for budget-conscious or quick fixes. Dentists often recommend implants when bone health matters.

Conclusion

Choose a dental bridge if you want a quick, affordable solution or if adjacent teeth already need crowns. Choose a dental implant if you prefer a permanent, natural solution and want to preserve bone. Final choice depends on budget, bone health, and your dentist's advice.
