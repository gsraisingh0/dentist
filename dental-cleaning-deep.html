Scaling and Root Planing (Deep Cleaning)

Scaling and root planing, commonly known as deep dental cleaning, is an advanced dental procedure recommended for patients who have gum problems or signs of periodontal disease. Unlike regular dental cleaning, deep cleaning focuses on removing harmful bacteria from below the gum line, where infection usually begins.

This treatment helps stop the progression of gum disease and restores gum health.

What Is Scaling and Root Planing?

Scaling and root planing is a non-surgical periodontal treatment used to treat gum disease. The procedure involves deep cleaning of the teeth and roots to remove plaque, tartar, and bacteria that cause gum inflammation and infection.

Scaling removes plaque and tartar from the tooth surface and below the gum line. Root planing smooths the tooth roots to prevent bacteria from reattaching and to help gums heal properly.

Who Needs Scaling and Root Planing?

Deep dental cleaning is recommended for patients experiencing:

  • Gum bleeding while brushing or flossing
  • Swollen, red, or inflamed gums
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Gum recession
  • Deep gum pockets
  • Patients diagnosed with gingivitis or periodontitis

Your dentist may recommend scaling and root planing after a detailed dental examination and gum pocket measurement.

What Is Included in Scaling and Root Planing?

  1. Cleaning Below the Gum Line — Special dental instruments are used to remove plaque and tartar buildup from beneath the gums, where regular brushing cannot reach.
  2. Smoothing of Tooth Roots — Root planing smooths rough areas of the tooth roots, making it harder for bacteria to attach and multiply.
  3. Infection Control — By removing bacteria and toxins, deep cleaning helps reduce inflammation and control gum infection.
  4. Local Anesthesia (If Required) — In some cases, local anesthesia may be used to ensure patient comfort, especially if the gums are sensitive or the infection is severe.

Benefits of Scaling and Root Planing

  • Treats gum disease effectively
  • Reduces gum bleeding and inflammation
  • Prevents further bone and tooth loss
  • Helps gums reattach to teeth
  • Controls bacterial infection
  • Improves overall oral health

Is Deep Cleaning Painful?

Scaling and root planing is usually well tolerated. Mild discomfort may be felt during or after the procedure, but local anesthesia ensures a comfortable experience. Any post-treatment sensitivity typically subsides within a few days.

How Long Does Scaling and Root Planing Take?

Deep cleaning may be completed in one or multiple visits, depending on the severity of gum disease and the number of affected areas.

Aftercare Tips Following Deep Dental Cleaning

  • Maintain good oral hygiene
  • Brush and floss regularly
  • Use prescribed mouthwash if advised
  • Avoid smoking
  • Attend follow-up dental visits

Conclusion

Scaling and root planing is a highly effective treatment for gum disease and is essential for controlling infection and preventing tooth loss. If you experience gum bleeding, swelling, or signs of periodontitis, deep dental cleaning can help restore your oral health and protect your smile.
