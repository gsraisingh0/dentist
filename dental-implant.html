Dental Implants

Dental Implant Treatment in Delhi for Permanent Tooth Replacement

A dental implant is an artificial tooth root that is placed into your jawbone to hold a replacement tooth or bridge. It looks, feels, and works like a natural tooth. Implants are usually made of titanium, a strong and biocompatible metal that fuses well with bone.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the most advanced and reliable solution for replacing missing teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants are permanently anchored to your jawbone, providing stability and comfort that feels just like natural teeth.

Key Benefits:

  • ✓ Permanent Solution: Lasts 15-25+ years or lifetime
  • ✓ Natural Appearance: Looks and feels like real teeth
  • ✓ Bone Preservation: Prevents jawbone deterioration
  • ✓ No Adjacent Damage: Doesn't harm nearby healthy teeth
  • ✓ Improved Confidence: Enjoy eating and speaking freely

Parts of a Dental Implant

  • Implant Fixture (Screw): The titanium post surgically inserted into the jawbone acting as the root.
  • Abutment: A small connector that attaches the crown to the implant fixture.
  • Crown: The visible part of the tooth — made of ceramic or porcelain — that looks like a real tooth.

Procedure (Step-by-Step)

  1. Initial Examination & X-ray: Dentist checks bone condition, gum health, and takes scans.
  2. Tooth Extraction (if needed): Damaged or decayed tooth is removed.
  3. Implant Placement: The titanium screw is surgically placed into the jawbone.
  4. Healing Period (Osseointegration): Bone grows around the implant — this takes 3 to 6 months.
  5. Abutment Placement: After healing, the abutment is attached.
  6. Crown Placement: A custom-made crown is placed on top to complete the implant.

Time Required

The full process may take 3 to 9 months, depending on bone healing speed, number of implants, and implant type.

  • Eat soft food for a few days after surgery

1. Types of Dental Implants

3 Main Types of Dental Implants:

1. Endosteal Implants

✔ Placed directly into jawbone
✔ Most common type
✔ Best for healthy bone
✔ Long-lasting & strong

2. Subperiosteal Implants

✔ Placed under gum, above jawbone
✔ Suitable for low bone density
✔ Custom-made design
✔ Alternative option

3. Zygomatic Implants

✔ Anchored in cheekbone
✔ Used in severe bone loss
✔ Advanced procedure
✔ Specialized dentist required

Other Dental Implant Types:

  • Single Tooth Implant: Replaces one missing tooth
  • Immediate Implant: Placed same day as tooth extraction
  • Basal Implant: Different design for bone integration
  • All-on-4 Implants: 4 implants support full mouth of teeth
  • Full Mouth Implants: Multiple implants for complete arch replacement

8. Dental Implant Procedure – Step by Step

Complete Implant Surgery Timeline:

  1. Initial Consultation (Week 1):
    • Detailed dental examination
    • X-ray and CBCT scan
    • Bone density assessment
    • Cost estimation and treatment plan
  2. Pre-Surgery Preparation (Week 2-3):
    • Tooth extraction (if required)
    • Bone graft (if needed) – wait 4-6 months for healing
    • Pre-operative blood tests
  3. Implant Screw Placement (Day of surgery):
    • Local anesthesia applied
    • Surgical incision in gum
    • Titanium screw placed into jawbone
    • Gum sutured
    • ⏱ Surgery duration: 30–60 minutes per implant
  4. Osseointegration Healing Period (3-6 months):
    • Bone fuses with implant
    • Temporary crown may be placed
    • Regular follow-up visits
    • Avoid smoking and hard foods
  5. Abutment Placement (Month 4-6):
    • Minor surgery to attach connector
    • Impression taken for custom crown
  6. Final Crown Placement (Month 6-9):
    • Custom-made crown attached
    • Bite adjustment and final polish
    • Patient-ready implant

Total Timeline:

3 to 9 months depending on bone healing speed, number of implants, and implant type.

Immediate Implants: Can complete in 3-4 months with advanced planning.

10. Dental Implant Side Effects & Risks

Common Temporary Side Effects (Normal):

  • Mild Swelling: Peak on 2nd-3rd day, resolves in 5-7 days
  • Temporary Pain/Discomfort: Controlled with pain medication
  • Gum Irritation: Minor inflammation, heals quickly
  • Slight Bleeding: Few drops for 1-2 days
  • Bruising: May appear, fades in 1-2 weeks

Serious Complications (Rare - Less than 2%):

  • Implant Failure: Bone doesn't integrate with implant (rare)
  • Infection: Can be treated with antibiotics
  • Nerve Damage: Extremely rare with modern techniques
  • Sinus Problems: Only if implant placed too high

Long-Term Risks:

  • Peri-implantitis: Implant gum disease (preventable with proper care)
  • Crown Damage: Repairable unlike implant itself
  • Bone Loss: Rare, managed through maintenance

Most implants last 15-25 years or entire lifetime with proper care.

11. Complete Dental Implant Aftercare Guide

First Week After Surgery:

  • Rest and avoid strenuous activities
  • Apply ice for 15 minutes every 2-3 hours (first 24 hours)
  • Take prescribed antibiotics and pain medication
  • Sleep with head elevated
  • Eat soft, cool foods (ice cream, yogurt, soup)
  • Avoid hot drinks and smoking completely

Weeks 2-4 (Healing Phase):

  • Gradually resume normal diet
  • Gentle oral hygiene (avoid implant area)
  • No smoking or alcohol
  • Continue prescribed medications
  • Attend follow-up appointments

Long-Term Care (Lifetime):

  • Daily brushing: 2-3 times with soft toothbrush
  • Flossing: Daily around implant
  • Use implant-specific oral care: Special floss/brushes
  • Regular dental visits: Every 6 months
  • Avoid smoking: Significantly increases implant failure
  • Avoid hard foods: Don't chew ice or hard candies
  • Use night guard: If you grind teeth
  • Maintain healthy diet: Good nutrition aids healing

Warning Signs - Contact Dentist If:

  • Swelling persists beyond 1 week
  • Severe pain not relieved by medication
  • Fever or signs of infection
  • Implant feels loose or moves
  • Excessive bleeding or discharge
