Orthodontic Treatment Cost in India – Complete Price Guide

Orthodontic treatment cost in India varies depending on the type of treatment, complexity of the dental problem, duration, and the orthodontist's expertise. With modern options like braces and clear aligners, patients can choose a treatment that fits both their dental needs and budget. This guide explains orthodontic treatment cost in India, price comparison between braces and aligners, and available EMI options.

Orthodontic Treatment Cost in India

The cost of orthodontic treatment in India generally ranges between:

₹25,000 to ₹3,50,000

The final price depends on:

  • Type of orthodontic treatment
  • Severity of alignment issues
  • Treatment duration
  • Clinic location and technology used

Braces Cost in India

Braces are the most commonly used and affordable orthodontic treatment option.

Types of Braces & Price Range

Metal Braces Cost

₹25,000 – ₹50,000

  • Most affordable option
  • Suitable for all orthodontic cases

Ceramic Braces Cost

₹40,000 – ₹80,000

  • Tooth-colored and less visible
  • Popular among adults and teenagers

Lingual Braces Cost

₹80,000 – ₹1,50,000

  • Placed behind the teeth
  • Completely invisible

Self-Ligating Braces Cost

₹60,000 – ₹1,20,000

  • Faster treatment and more comfort
  • Advanced bracket technology

Clear Aligners Cost in India (Invisalign & Others)

Clear aligners are a premium orthodontic solution designed for aesthetics and comfort.

Clear Aligners Price Range

₹80,000 – ₹3,50,000

Invisalign Cost in India

₹1,50,000 – ₹3,50,000

Depends on number of aligners and case complexity

Clear aligners are ideal for patients who want a discreet and removable orthodontic treatment.

Braces Cost vs Aligners – Comparison

Feature Braces Clear Aligners
Cost Lower Higher
Visibility Visible Nearly invisible
Removable No Yes
Treatment Complexity Suitable for all cases Best for mild–moderate cases
Comfort Moderate High
Maintenance More care needed Easy to clean

Braces are best for complex cases, while aligners are preferred for comfort and aesthetics.

Factors Affecting Orthodontic Treatment Cost

  • Type of orthodontic appliance
  • Duration of treatment
  • Number of follow-up visits
  • Additional procedures (extractions, expanders)
  • Retainers after treatment

An orthodontist can provide an exact cost after clinical examination.

EMI Options for Orthodontic Treatment

Many dental clinics in India offer easy EMI options to make orthodontic treatment affordable.

Common EMI Facilities

  • Monthly installment plans
  • Zero or low-interest EMI
  • Credit card / debit card EMI
  • Clinic-based flexible payment plans

EMI options allow patients to start treatment without financial stress.

Is Orthodontic Treatment Worth the Cost?

Orthodontic treatment is a long-term investment in oral health and confidence. Benefits include:

  • Improved dental hygiene
  • Reduced risk of cavities and gum disease
  • Better bite and chewing function
  • Enhanced smile and self-confidence

Properly aligned teeth can prevent expensive dental problems in the future.

How to Get an Accurate Cost Estimate?

To know the exact orthodontic treatment cost:

  1. Schedule a consultation with an orthodontist
  2. Get a dental examination and X-rays
  3. Discuss treatment options and payment plans

Conclusion

Orthodontic treatment cost in India is flexible and depends on the type of braces or aligners chosen. With affordable braces, advanced clear aligners, and easy EMI options, achieving a straight and healthy smile is now accessible for everyone.

Consult a qualified orthodontist to choose the right treatment option that fits your dental needs and budget.
